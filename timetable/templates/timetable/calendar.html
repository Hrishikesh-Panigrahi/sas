How to add it

  1. Google Calendar: press Add to Google Calendar and confirm with Add. On a phone, do this once in a browser; the calendar then shows up in the Google Calendar app.
  2. iPhone, iPad or Mac: press Apple / Outlook and choose Subscribe.
  3. Outlook on the web: choose Add calendar › Subscribe from web and paste the calendar link.

Calendar apps check for changes every few hours; Google can take up to a day. Download .ics gives you a one-off copy that won't update.
